How to make yellow cake

This recipe is for baking better yellow cake from the cake mixes. You can also try to bake the cake from scratch but in my opinion this is quicker.

    Ingredients:
  • Yellow Cake Mix (Preferably 15.25 oz Pillsbury Yellow Cake)
  • Buttermilk
  • Butter (1 stick)
  • Sourcream
  • 3 eggs
  1. First, you want to mix eggs, buttermilk, sourcrea and butter into a bowl
  2. Add cake mix and stir just enough so that its not completely smooth
  3. Use non-stick spray if you don't have a non stick pan and pour the batter. Follow the baking instructions from the mix package

Your Cake is done! Enjoy.
