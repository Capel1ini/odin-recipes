How to make lasagna

If you want try your hand at making lasagna then you should absolutely try this recipe. Let me go straight to the point

    Ingredients:
  • Pasta sheets - 5 sheets
  • Garlic (1/2 cup)
  • Onions (1/2 cup)
  • Tomato Basil Sauce
  1. First, you want to boil lasagna sheets. Fill a pan with 2 cups of water and boil sheets for 7 minutes or until desired doneness
  2. Prepare the meat sauce: Fill a large pot with garlic, onion, chilli powder, sausage, beef, a bit of water and some tomato basil sauce. Stir at medium heat for 10 minutes.
  3. Once the sauce and sheets are ready, layer them on each other alternately, then bake them at 350F for at least 15 minutes

Your lasagna is done! Enjoy.
